# WarmPath — Competitive Strategy

> **Sunset notice:** This project was sunset on April 28, 2026.

## Market Position (at sunset)

WarmPath operated in the employee-referral marketplace space. The core thesis:
cold applications convert at 1-3%, but employee referrals convert at 10-40%.
Nobody owned the "get me referred" workflow as a product.

## Competitive Landscape

| Competitor Type | Examples | WarmPath Differentiator |
|---|---|---|
| Job boards | LinkedIn, Indeed | Referral-first vs. application-first |
| Networking tools | Lunchclub, Shapr | Transactional (credits) vs. social |
| ATS/recruiting | Greenhouse, Lever | Candidate-side vs. employer-side |
| Referral platforms | Teamable, Drafted | Two-sided marketplace vs. employer tool |

## Key Differentiators

1. **Privacy architecture** — contacts never exposed without explicit approval
2. **Cultural context** — AI adapts intro style to relationship and culture
3. **Credit economy** — network holders monetize dormant referral bonuses
4. **Two-sided** — serves both job seekers and network holders

## Pricing (at sunset)

- Job seekers: credit-based, ~$20-30/month equivalent
- Network holders: free (earn credits + referral bonuses)
- Free tier: limited searches, no intro requests
